Open Pilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Open Pilot ("the app") is an open-source chat client for iOS. It is designed to be private by architecture: there is no Open Pilot backend, no account, and no server operated by us that receives your data. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent to third parties you choose to connect to.

1. Data stored on your device

All of the following are stored only on your iPhone and are never transmitted to us:

  • Conversations and messages (local database).
  • Projects, custom instructions, and automatic memory facts.
  • App settings and preferences.
  • Provider API keys, which are stored in the iOS Keychain (encrypted, device-only).

Deleting the app removes this data. We cannot access, recover, or read it.

2. No data collected by us

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no telemetry. We do not operate any server that your device communicates with.

3. Third-party services you connect to

The app sends your prompts directly from your device to the model providers you configure. Those providers process your requests under their own privacy policies. We are not a party to that exchange and do not intercept it.

  • Model providers you add in Settings (for example a server on your local network, or cloud APIs such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Alibaba DashScope). Your messages, and any images or files you attach, are sent to the provider you selected so it can generate a reply.
  • Web search (optional): search queries are sent to DuckDuckGo to retrieve results.
  • Weather (optional): location or place names are sent to Open-Meteo.
  • Currency (optional): currency pairs are sent to Frankfurter.

You control which providers are configured and can remove them at any time. API keys go directly to the provider you entered; they are never sent to us.

4. Microphone, speech, and camera

Voice input uses Apple's on-device Speech framework to convert speech to text, and text-to-speech uses Apple's on-device synthesizer. Audio is processed on your device and is not sent to us. Camera and photo access are used only to attach images you choose to send to your configured provider. These permissions are optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, since it collects no data at all.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue in the public repository: github.com/ELPilotPR/open-pilot.
